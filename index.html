I am a Researcher in the Internet Architecture (INET) group at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics, Saarbrücken, Germany, advised by Prof. Anja Feldmann. I completed my PhD at National Institute of Informatics (NII) / Graduate University for Advanced Studies, SOKENDAI under the supervision of Prof. Kensuke Fukuda.

My research focuses on online tracking, web privacy, and network measurement. The modern web economy is powered by an advertising ecosystem that relies on sophisticated cross-site tracking — yet the real-world effectiveness of privacy protections (browser safeguards, regulatory interventions, platform policies) remains an open empirical question, as tracking techniques continuously adapt in response to defenses.

I study this cat-and-mouse dynamic through scalable, automated web measurement: building frameworks to detect and analyze tracking mechanisms at scale, and evaluating whether privacy countermeasures meaningfully limit them in practice. My goal is to improve transparency and accountability in the web's data collection ecosystem — producing evidence that informs better privacy protections for researchers, policymakers, and platform designers.
